Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Because our products are digital downloads delivered instantly, a purchase cannot be cancelled once payment is complete. We do give refunds in these cases:

  • You were charged but could not download the file, and we could not fix it within 3 working days.
  • You were charged twice for the same purchase.
  • The product is materially different from its description on this website.

Request a refund within 7 days of purchase through the Contact page, with your payment ID. Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.

We do not refund because of results (views, subscribers or income), or because a third-party service such as Claude, YouTube or Pexels changed its terms or prices.
