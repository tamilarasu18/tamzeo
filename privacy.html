Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

What we collect

When you buy, Razorpay collects the details needed for the payment (such as your email, phone number and payment method) and shares with us the email, phone number, amount and payment status. If you email us, we receive your message and email address.

How we use it

To deliver your purchase, answer support requests, process refunds and keep records required by law. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Payment data

Card, UPI and bank details are handled by Razorpay under its own privacy policy. We never receive them.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Retention

Payment records are kept as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires. Support emails are kept for up to 2 years.

Your choices

You can ask us to see or delete your information, except records we must keep by law, through the Contact page.
